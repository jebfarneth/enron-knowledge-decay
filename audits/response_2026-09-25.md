# Response to the 2026-09-25 data audit

The audit ([report](data_audit_codex_2026-09-25.md), evidence in
[`evidence/`](evidence/)) reviewed commit `8c595e7` and concluded the data
layer was not yet fit for Phase 3. Each finding is listed below with the
commit that addresses it, the effect on the data, and what remains open.
All numbers come from the full rerun in commit `f5cb3f4`.

Status: **fixed** (the demonstrated defect is corrected and has a test),
**partly fixed** (corrected in part, remainder listed), **open** (documented,
not yet corrected).

## Network and evaluation

| Finding | Status | Commit | Change and effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fractional weights do not neutralize broadcasts for degree | fixed (documentation), open (definition) | `14f43b1` | The docstring states that 1/n weights affect only the weighted measures. ## Threads

|---|---|---|---|
| Inferred reply links are not validated responses | partly fixed | `107a10e`, `08a778d` | Links need the reply to be addressed back or to quote the parent; the parent quoted nearest the top wins; causally inverted pairs are rejected; automated and structured messages are never linked; forwards are labelled separately. ## Before Phase 3 text models

The audit's leakage table (section 5) stands as the checklist: redact
signatures, names and contact details and report the ablations; add
title-keyword-only and identity-only baselines; split by person and by date;
keep near-duplicate families and threads within one split; rebuild templates
and thresholds from pre-cutoff data for any temporal claim.
